# My Product Review

**Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ (4/5)
**Price:** $60
**Date Reviewed:** 2026-08-21

## Overview

Hogwarts Legacy is an immersive, open-world action RPG.

## Pros

- You can ride a broom.
- You can learn different magical spells.
- The storyline is exciting.

## Cons

- Once you chose a house, you have to finish the story in that house. The only other option is to start a new slot in a different house of your choice.
- The customization of your character is lacking options.

## Performance

The graphics and the storyline are high quality.

## Value for Money

I think it's worth the money, although I have to admit that I got it for free due to a cpecial christmas offer.

## Verdict

I haven't finished the game yet but I absolutely will. And I know for a fact that I will play the whole game again, hopefully experimenting with some in-game add-ons.
